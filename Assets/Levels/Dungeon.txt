// 2: the Starlight Wand, the prize of the locked treasure room.
// The dungeon under the castle: a labyrinth of rooms and winding tunnels.
//   The storeroom (barrels and crates) is through the door and down the hall east of the start room.
//   Bonesy, a friendly skeleton, keeps a campfire going south of the start (warm up there!).
//   The great puddle hall has slimes splashing about, and a quiet mushroom grotto off its east
//   side; the Slime King (M) waits in the big hall below it.
//   The Rusty Key (y) hides at the far end of the twisty tunnels under the campfire room; it opens
//   the locked door (k) to the treasure room past the storeroom.
// Hazards: spike traps (^) in the tunnel below the campfire room and in the corridor to the
//   treasure room (they ripple from west to east, so follow the wave), and two lava pools (~)
//   in the Slime King's hall: mind his Ground Slam's knockback!
// The Slime King leaves the Amethyst (3) and the Bouncy Boots (4) where he fell; the stairs (X) behind
//   his hall lead back up to the castle grounds (arriving by the stairs there).
// A fake wall (0) in the east wall of the start room looks like the rest of the wall, but you can walk
//   through it: behind it is Dad's Workshop, with a computer, a signed note and a star shard.
// Gaps (5) you can't cross yet, so come back wearing the Bouncy Boots: a one-tile gap above the first corridor
//   (a star shard), and a two-tile gap through the east wall of the Slime King's hall (an egg: one of
//   Amethyra's five). Two braziers (6) in the mushroom grotto: light both with magic and a star shard appears.
